Acme Capital Partners — Q2 FY2026 Internal Quarterly Report

Prepared for internal distribution. Not for external release.

Revenue Summary
Total revenue for Q2 FY2026 was $171.2 million, up from $148.9 million in
Q2 FY2025, a year-over-year increase of 15.0%.

Segment breakdown:
- Asset Management fees: $91.7 million (54% of total revenue)
- Advisory fees: $48.3 million (28% of total revenue)
- Trading and principal transactions: $31.2 million (18% of total revenue)

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $118.9 million, compared to $106.2 million in
the prior-year quarter, an increase of 12.0%. Compensation expense accounted
for $79.4 million of the total.

Net Income
Net income for the quarter was $39.1 million, compared to $30.4 million in
Q2 FY2025 — an increase of 28.6%. Diluted earnings per share were $1.16,
compared to $0.91 in the prior-year quarter.

Balance Sheet Highlights
Total assets under management (AUM) reached $38.1 billion at quarter end, up
from $36.4 billion at the end of Q1 FY2026. Cash and cash equivalents stood
at $547 million.

Outlook
Management expects advisory revenue to accelerate in Q3 as several mandates
signed late in Q2 reach completion. Asset management fee growth is expected
to track net inflows, which totaled $1.2 billion for the quarter.

Note on Comparability
Q2 FY2026 results include a one-time $3.8 million charge related to the
office consolidation in London. Excluding this charge, operating expenses
would have been $115.1 million and net income $42.9 million.
