Acme Capital Partners — Q1 FY2026 Internal Quarterly Report

Prepared for internal distribution. Not for external release.

Revenue Summary
Total revenue for Q1 FY2026 was $158.4 million, up from $141.7 million in
Q1 FY2025, a year-over-year increase of 11.8%.

Segment breakdown:
- Asset Management fees: $88.9 million (56% of total revenue)
- Advisory fees: $41.6 million (26% of total revenue)
- Trading and principal transactions: $27.9 million (18% of total revenue)

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $112.5 million, compared to $101.3 million in
the prior-year quarter, an increase of 11.1%. Compensation expense accounted
for $74.8 million of the total.

Net Income
Net income for the quarter was $33.7 million, compared to $28.1 million in
Q1 FY2025 — an increase of 19.9%. Diluted earnings per share were $1.00,
compared to $0.84 in the prior-year quarter.

Balance Sheet Highlights
Total assets under management (AUM) reached $36.4 billion at quarter end, up
from $35.2 billion at the end of Q4 FY2025. Cash and cash equivalents stood
at $498 million.

Outlook
Management expects advisory revenue to remain broadly flat in Q2 given a
thin mandate pipeline entering the quarter, with asset management fees
carrying growth. Net inflows for the quarter were $0.9 billion.

Risk Note
The Chief Risk Officer flagged that transaction monitoring alert volume grew
34% year over year while headcount in the Compliance operations team was
unchanged. Remediation options are to be presented to the Board in Q2.
