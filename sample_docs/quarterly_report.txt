Acme Capital Partners — Q3 FY2026 Internal Quarterly Report

Prepared for internal distribution. Not for external release.

Revenue Summary
Total revenue for Q3 FY2026 was $184.6 million, up from $152.3 million in
Q3 FY2025, a year-over-year increase of 21.2%.

Segment breakdown:
- Asset Management fees: $96.2 million (52% of total revenue)
- Advisory fees: $58.1 million (31% of total revenue)
- Trading and principal transactions: $30.3 million (17% of total revenue)

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $121.4 million, compared to $108.7 million in
the prior-year quarter, an increase of 11.7%. The increase was primarily
driven by higher compensation expense and continued investment in the
compliance and risk technology platform.

Net Income
Net income for the quarter was $47.8 million, compared to $31.2 million in
Q3 FY2025 — an increase of 53.2%. Diluted earnings per share were $1.42,
compared to $0.94 in the prior-year quarter.

Balance Sheet Highlights
Total assets under management (AUM) reached $42.3 billion at quarter end, up
from $38.1 billion at the end of Q2 FY2026. Cash and cash equivalents stood
at $612 million.

Outlook
Management expects continued growth in advisory fee revenue in Q4, driven by
a strong pipeline of mid-market M&A mandates, while asset management fee
growth is expected to moderate slightly given recent market volatility.
