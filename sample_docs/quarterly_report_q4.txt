Acme Capital Partners — Q4 FY2026 Internal Quarterly Report

Classification: Internal — Acme Capital Partners Staff Only. Not for external release.

Revenue Summary
Total revenue for Q4 FY2026 was $198.2 million, up from $164.5 million in Q4 FY2025,
representing a year-over-year increase of 20.5%.

Segment breakdown:
- Asset Management fees: $104.5 million (52.7% of total revenue)
- Advisory fees: $62.4 million (31.5% of total revenue)
- Trading and principal transactions: $31.3 million (15.8% of total revenue)

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $128.6 million, compared to $114.2 million in the
prior-year quarter, an increase of 12.6%. Growth in expenses was driven by annual
technology infrastructure investments and expanding compliance risk oversight.

Net Income
Net income for the quarter reached $52.4 million, compared to $35.8 million in
Q4 FY2025 — a year-over-year increase of 46.4%. Diluted earnings per share were $1.56,
compared to $1.08 in the prior-year quarter.

Balance Sheet Highlights
Total assets under management (AUM) reached $45.1 billion at year end FY2026, up from
$42.3 billion in Q3 FY2026. Cash and cash equivalents stood at $648 million with zero
outstanding debt under the revolving credit facility.

Full Year FY2026 Summary
Total full-year revenue reached $715.4 million (+19.8% YoY) with full-year net income
of $181.2 million. Operating margin expanded by 180 basis points across all segments.
