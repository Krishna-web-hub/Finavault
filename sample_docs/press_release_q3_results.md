# Acme Capital Partners Reports Third Quarter FY2026 Results

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — Public information, approved for external distribution.**

BOSTON — Acme Capital Partners today announced financial results for the
third quarter of fiscal year 2026.

## Highlights

- Total revenue of **$184.6 million**, an increase of 21.2% year over year.
- Net income of **$47.8 million**, an increase of 53.2% year over year.
- Diluted earnings per share of **$1.42**, compared to $0.94 a year earlier.
- Assets under management of **$42.3 billion** at quarter end.

## Management Commentary

"Our advisory business had an exceptional quarter, and our asset management
franchise continued to attract net inflows in a volatile market," said
Marianne Alvarez, Chief Executive Officer. "We remain focused on disciplined
growth and on continued investment in our compliance and risk platform."

Chief Financial Officer David Okonkwo added that the firm ended the quarter
with $612 million in cash and cash equivalents and no outstanding borrowings
under its revolving credit facility.

## About Acme Capital Partners

Acme Capital Partners is an independent asset management and advisory firm
founded in 1998 and headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, with offices in
New York, London, and Singapore. Acme Securities LLC, its broker-dealer
subsidiary, is a member of FINRA and SIPC.
