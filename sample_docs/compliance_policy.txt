Acme Capital Partners — Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Customer Due
Diligence Policy

Classification: Confidential — Compliance and Legal personnel only.

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the minimum standards for customer due diligence
(CDD) and enhanced due diligence (EDD) applicable to all client onboarding
and ongoing monitoring activities at Acme Capital Partners, in accordance
with applicable AML regulations.

2. Customer Due Diligence Requirements
All new clients must undergo identity verification prior to account opening,
including:
- Government-issued photo identification
- Proof of address dated within the last 90 days
- Source-of-funds documentation for deposits exceeding $50,000
- Beneficial ownership disclosure for all entity clients (25% threshold)

3. Enhanced Due Diligence Triggers
EDD procedures are required when a client meets any of the following
criteria:
- Politically Exposed Person (PEP) status, or immediate family of a PEP
- Country of residence or incorporation on the firm's high-risk jurisdiction
  list
- Cash-intensive business as the primary source of funds
- Transaction volume inconsistent with the client's stated profile within
  any 30-day period

4. Ongoing Monitoring
Client accounts are subject to automated transaction monitoring with alerts
escalated to the Compliance team within 24 hours. Suspicious Activity
Reports (SARs) must be filed within the regulatory deadline from the date an
alert is escalated, per jurisdictional requirements.

5. Recordkeeping
All CDD/EDD documentation must be retained for a minimum of seven years
following account closure.
