Prepared by: Priya Raghunathan, Chief Compliance Officer

Subject Account
Client Account #48213 is a Family Office relationship domiciled in Panama,
opened 2017-01-14, holding approximately $61.4 million under a Liquid
Alternatives mandate. The account carries a High internal risk rating and a
politically exposed person (PEP) flag.

Activity Under Review
Between 2026-06-02 and 2026-06-14, the account originated three outbound
wire transfers totalling $2.4 million, each routed through intermediary
institutions in a jurisdiction appearing on the firm's high-risk list. The
transfers were inconsistent with the stated investment mandate, which
contemplates no third-party disbursements.

Basis for Suspicion
1. Structuring pattern — each transfer fell below the $1 million internal
   secondary-review threshold.
2. Counterparty opacity — two beneficiaries could not be verified against
   the beneficial ownership disclosure on file.
3. Timing — transfers began four days after a routine periodic review closed.

Recommended Action
File within the statutory 30-day window. Outside counsel has been engaged.
Account activity restrictions have been applied pending resolution. No
communication regarding this review may be made to the client or to any
person outside the Compliance function and Executive Committee.
