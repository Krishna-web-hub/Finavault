Acme Capital Partners — Incident Post-Mortem

Classification: Confidential — Technology, Risk, and Compliance personnel.

Incident: INC-2026-0412 — Delayed transaction monitoring alerts
Severity: SEV-2
Date of incident: 2026-04-12
Author: Samuel Whitfield, Head of Risk Technology

Summary
Between 03:20 and 14:55 UTC on 2026-04-12, the automated transaction
monitoring pipeline processed alerts with a delay averaging 6.5 hours
against a 24-hour escalation commitment. No alert was missed, and no
regulatory deadline was breached, but 31 alerts were escalated to the
Compliance team later than the internal 4-hour target.

Root Cause
A schema migration deployed on 2026-04-11 added a column to the alerts table
without a corresponding index. The nightly batch job that scores alerts by
risk performs a lookup against that column for every row; without the index
the query degraded from an index scan to a sequential scan, and batch
runtime grew from 18 minutes to just over 6 hours.

Contributing Factors
- The migration was reviewed for correctness but not for query plan impact.
- Batch runtime had no alerting threshold; the regression was discovered
  when a Compliance analyst asked why the morning queue was empty.
- The staging dataset is roughly 2% of production volume, so the sequential
  scan completed in seconds there and the regression was invisible.

Remediation
1. Index added on the new column; batch runtime returned to 19 minutes.
   (Completed 2026-04-12)
2. Batch runtime alert at 45 minutes, paging Risk Technology on breach.
   (Completed 2026-04-19)
3. Migration review checklist now requires an EXPLAIN plan for any query
   touching a modified table. (Completed 2026-05-03)
4. Staging dataset volume increased to 25% of production. (Target 2026-Q4,
   owner: Platform Engineering)

Regulatory Assessment
Compliance reviewed all 31 delayed alerts. None resulted in a Suspicious
Activity Report filing deadline being missed. Outside counsel advised that
no regulator notification was required. The incident is recorded in the
operational risk register as ORR-2026-118.
